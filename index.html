The house

Old spices,
a quieter table.

SavorySpot is a pure vegetarian kitchen in Bandra East. Idli and sambar in the morning, paneer makhani and a steel thali at lunch, veg kebabs and a full thali after dark.

No meat. No fish. No egg. Ever. Tell us if you eat Jain, and the pass will hold the onion and garlic.
