About SavorySpot

SavorySpot is a modern restaurant in Bandra East built around slow cooking, generous spices, and a room that feels like evening even at noon.

We source daily from local markets, keep a mostly vegetarian kitchen with coastal and Punjabi plates, and treat every booking as a personal welcome.

  • Farm-fresh produce, milled spices
  • Private tables for 2–12 guests
  • Walk-ins welcome until 10:30 PM
