The house

Old spices,
a quieter table.

SavorySpot is a pure vegetarian kitchen in Bandra East. We cook from the grocer’s morning crate — millet idli, kanda poha, tandoor paneer, dum biryani in clay, and a brass thali for those who want the full house.

No meat. No fish. No egg. Ever. Tell us if you eat Jain, and the pass will hold the onion and garlic.
